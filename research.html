Research

My current work studies how priors and partial observations can guide robotic perception, search and manipulation. My broader direction is to develop resource-efficient, adaptable robots for long-horizon mobile manipulation.

Research themes

Current foundation

Sparse world models and uncertainty

Object-centric scene graphs, uncertainty-aware perception and persistent beliefs for reasoning from partial observations.

Research direction

Resource-efficient robot learning

Structured knowledge and prior experience that reduce the data, computation and interaction needed to learn new tasks.

Research direction

Adaptable mobile manipulation

Robots that update their understanding and behavior across objects, tasks and environments over long horizons.

Research direction

I aim to develop robots that make efficient use of prior knowledge, limited observations and their own experience. The broader goal is adaptable mobile manipulation: systems that learn selectively, revise their understanding when needed and remain effective as objects, tasks and environments change.

Selected work

O-STaR: Open-Vocabulary Object Search through Spatio-Temporal Reasoning on Dynamic Scene Graphs

GRC 2026 · Cologne, Germany

O-STaR searches for relocated and concealed objects by grounding language-model priors in scene geometry and updating beliefs on a dynamic scene graph. It was validated on Stretch and in 60-day simulated scenarios; an extended version is under review at ICRA 2027.

Paper

SG-DOR: Learning Scene Graphs with Direction-Conditioned Occlusion Reasoning for Pepper Plants

IROS 2026 · Pittsburgh, USA

SG-DOR uses direction-conditioned scene graphs to infer plant-part attachments and rank leaves that obstruct a pepper from a chosen approach direction. It achieved 0.73 occlusion F1 and 0.85 NDCG@3.

EvidMTL: Evidential Multi-Task Learning for Uncertainty-Aware Semantic Surface Mapping from Monocular RGB Images

IROS 2025 · Hangzhou, China

EvidMTL introduces evidential depth and semantic heads with a new evidential depth loss that calibrates predictive belief strength from monocular RGB. EvidKimera uses these uncertainties for consistent 3D mapping and outperformed Kimera by 30% in zero-shot ScanNetV2 semantic surface mapping accuracy and consistency.

PaperarXivCodeCheckpoints

HortiBot: An Adaptive Multi-Arm System for Robotic Horticulture of Sweet Peppers

IROS 2024 · Abu Dhabi, UAE

HortiBot’s perception stack performs real-time pepper, peduncle and stem detection, online data association, and scene-graph world modelling of pepper plants. The integrated three-arm system uses active perception and dual-arm manipulation for selective harvesting of real peppers.

DawnIK: Decentralized Collision-Aware Inverse Kinematics Solver for Heterogeneous Multi-Arm Systems

Humanoids 2023 · Austin, USA

DawnIK expresses inverse-kinematics and collision constraints as weighted costs in a nonlinear optimizer, without requiring inverse-Jacobian computation or redundant degrees of freedom. Against CollisionIK, it produced no collisions in any evaluated multi-arm scenario and improved trajectory tracking when collision risk was low.

PaperarXivCode

NBV-SC: Next Best View Planning Based on Shape Completion for Fruit Mapping and Reconstruction

IROS 2023 · Detroit, USA

NBV-SC synthesizes targeted views of predicted missing fruit surfaces and rejects redundant samples using viewpoint dissimilarity. It improved fruit-size estimation and reconstruction, reduced viewpoint-sampling time by 80–90% in the harder simulated scenarios, and was demonstrated with a UR5e in a commercial glasshouse.

Flexible, Semi-Autonomous Grasping for Assistive Robotics

ICRA 2016 · Stockholm, Sweden

The system combines a dense graspability map with dynamic virtual fixtures that adapt online to the operator’s commanded motion: the user controls translation while the system orients the hand and automates grasp closure. In a SpaceMouse user study, the semi-autonomous mode reduced task-completion time and subjective workload compared with pure teleoperation.

Open-Vocabulary and Semantic-Aware Reasoning for Search and Retrieval of Objects in Dynamic and Concealed Spaces

IROS 2025 Workshop · Hangzhou, China · Best Poster

Stretch-Compose combines spatial, semantic and geometric reasoning to retrieve relocated or concealed objects from an outdated map. Real-robot trials achieved 100% navigation and open-space detection while reducing concealed-space search time by 68%.

GO-VMP: Global Optimization for View Motion Planning in Fruit Mapping

IROS 2025 · Hangzhou, China

GO-VMP jointly optimizes camera viewpoints and collision-free motions to increase fruit coverage while limiting motion cost. It was evaluated in simulation and real-world experiments.
